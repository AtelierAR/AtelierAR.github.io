    Sightline study

    Every row is set out with the C-value formula, aimed at the near touchline at field level. Seated eye height is 3 ft 11 in. The mezzanine rail seats use a standing eye height of 5 ft 3 in.

    C-value is how far a spectator’s sightline passes above the eye of the person in the row in front. Reference values: 2.4 in (60 mm) minimum, 3.5 in (90 mm) good, 4.7 in (120 mm) excellent. Far-corner distance is measured from the back row to the opposite corner of the field, end zone included.
